Main Cave tours 12 printable add-ons he uses with a Bambu P1S, noting that many also fit the P2S and other printers. His strongest recommendations are a riser to keep the lid from striking the glass, an auxiliary-fan diffuser to reduce edge lifting, and a poop chute; the rest focus on filament storage, organization, tool storage, and cosmetics.

What this video offers

Match spool material to the filament's drying temperature: PLA spools are fine for PLA but can warp when drying PETG; use PETG for a spool that will hold PETG in a dryer.

Print the riser, vent cover, and auxiliary-fan diffuser in PETG when they sit inside or near the heated printer. The creator says PLA can warp or melt there.

He calls the riser, fan diffuser, and poop holder the most essential prints. The plate holder, labels, tool holders, door-screw caps, and filament gauge are primarily convenience or cosmetic upgrades.

The filament measuring stick is only an approximate guide, not a precise scale.
