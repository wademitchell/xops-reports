🤖

Nion Anarchy tried a month of long sleep and background-sound videos, then added deliberately ridiculous sound loops. He reports 7,307 views, 253 new subscribers and $9.47 in revenue: the title's promise of generational profits is the joke. A Toyota Corolla struggling to start became the standout, while ordinary rain videos mostly struggled.

Source basis

Full-video Gemini viewing notes, including the final results and ending, rather than verbatim captions; caption retrieval failed. Description supplied chapter links. Resource homepages were independently checked; channel identity matches the description's Santai link.

Key ideas

The premise: sleeping listeners might leave a video running for hours. He starts with an existing, already monetized channel with about 1,700 subscribers, rebrands it as Santai, and initially disables mid-video ads so they do not wake listeners. This is not a test of starting from zero. Intro and setup

Research and production: he looks for searches with demand but relatively little competition, choosing rain-cabin sounds over broad rain searches. His first 10-hour video combines rain recordings with an original Blender cabin scene, edited in Premiere Pro. It takes about five hours to build the scene and four hours to export; the roughly 70 GB file and his internet data cap make uploading another obstacle. Making the first video

Week 1, rain: he switches to stock footage to save time, tests thumbnail variants, and adds translated titles and descriptions. Rain on roofs, cars and tents, soft drizzle, a black-screen thunderstorm and a cyberpunk rain scene bring little traction. Reported cumulative results: 152 views, 3 subscribers gained and $0.14. Days 4-7

Week 2, nature: forest rain, a creek and waterfall, ocean waves, an outdoor tent and a blizzard do better. He changes the plan to pursue the stronger forest theme rather than sticking to desert dunes. Reported cumulative results: 785 views, 14 subscribers gained and $1.05. Week 2

Week 3, everyday objects: he schedules radio static, cat purring, clock ticking and TV static while away. The earlier forest-rain video becomes the first to pass 1,000 views. Reported cumulative results: 2,102 views, 139 subscribers gained and $2.77. Week 3

Week 4, absurd sounds: chainsaws, construction and drilling are followed by a Corolla failing to start until 9:59:30, then a washing machine, mosquito and low-battery smoke alarm. The Corolla is the breakout: he reports 4,275 views and $5.97, and changes his approach by adding mid-video ad slots every two minutes. These are his reported figures, not independently verified earnings. Week 4

Creator's key callouts

Better packaging and a distinctive idea matter: he credits attractive forest imagery and the Corolla's clear joke more than generic rain footage or translated metadata. Conclusion

His attempted algorithm trick fails: trimming an existing 10-hour rain video to about three hours does not revive it. He concludes the old trick no longer works; one experiment does not establish how YouTube's recommendation system changed. The experiment

The month earns very little despite considerable production work. He leaves open the possibility that an older library might gain viewers later, rather than demonstrating a reliable income stream. Final results
