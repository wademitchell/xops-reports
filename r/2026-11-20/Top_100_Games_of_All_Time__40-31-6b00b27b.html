Top 100 Games of All Time: 40-31

Creator's ranking

Joey Evans, Wendy Yi, and Chris Yi each count down ten personal favorites, from #40 to #31. Joey’s choices often create memorable moments at the table, Wendy keeps coming back to games that let her build up to a big turn, and Chris likes games that make a familiar rule work in an unexpected way. Their #31 picks are Ethnos, Crusaders: Thy Will Be Done, and Azure, respectively.

Source basis

Full timestamped YouTube captions, including the final picks and wrap-up. The three hosts’ ranks are separate, not ties or a combined ranking. Game links are independently verified publisher or BoardGameGeek references, or the creator’s own review.

40

Joey: RUN

A two-player chase with a fugitive using gadgets and shortcuts while law enforcement searches by helicopter. Joey says it feels like a movie chase; he and his wife played about six times in a row. 7:24

40

Wendy: Incan Gold

Keep exploring for gems or walk out with what you’ve found before a hazard wipes it out. Wendy loves the suspense of trying to leave at the last possible moment. BoardGameGeek lists it as Diamant. 9:34

40

Chris: Tzolk’in: The Mayan Calendar

Rotating gears change what workers can do the longer you leave them in place. Chris likes how tight it plays at two, though getting the timing wrong can leave you behind. 11:16

boardgamegeek.com
39

Joey: Andromeda’s Edge

Send out ships, develop technology, and regroup to get ships back. Joey likes the distinct factions and quick fights; it fell from his top ten because it’s hard to get to the table. 13:59

boardgamegeek.com
39

Wendy: Concordia

Play action cards from your hand, then spend a turn picking them back up. Wendy loves building up her options and that a production action can help other players, too. 15:34

boardgamegeek.com
39

Chris: Lone Wolves

In this two-player card game, the loser of a trick gets a bonus token. Chris likes that sometimes the token is worth more than winning the trick. 16:50

Joey: Journeys Afar: The Ketsueki

Gates connect worlds with different encounters and activities. Joey likes taking big risks, and dying costs points rather than ending the adventure. The link is to his separate Dice Tower review. 19:11

Wendy: Rolled West

Roll dice for resources and mark the results on a sheet. Wendy likes being able to save resources between turns so she can plan a big turn instead of being stuck with a bad roll. 22:23

Chris: Vegetable Stock

Everyone takes a card; the leftover card moves vegetable prices. Chris likes how much game fits on one card’s worth of rules and how quickly it plays at six. 23:35

37

Joey: The Lord of the Rings: Duel for Middle-earth

A two-player card duel you can win through the Ring quest, alliances, or control of the map. Joey loves the components and different paths to victory. 25:25

Wendy: Cozy Stickerville

Build a village together with stickers and choices that branch as you play. Wendy keeps asking her family to replay it so they can see what happens if they build something different. 27:09

37

Chris: Raiders of the North Sea

Place one worker and pick up another to take two actions while preparing for raids. Chris finds that rhythm quick and distinctive, and praises the expansions. 28:43

36

Joey: Terrorscape

Survivors sneak through a mansion while a killer hunts them. Joey loves the tension of trying to repair the radio and that the big mansion helps conceal movement rather than just looking impressive. 31:25

36

Wendy: Viticulture Essential Edition

Wendy: Three Sisters: Harvest Edition

Chris: Bärenpark

Play bands of cards to control regions, knowing opponents can use what you discard. Joey prefers the original’s fantasy powers, readable map, and direct competition for territory. 1:06:44

Wendy: Crusaders: Thy Will Be Done

Chris: Azure
