The creator tours 13 newer 3D-printing tools spanning AI-assisted Gridfinity organizers, model-texture and color workflows, mobile and AI slicers, and filament databases. His strongest practical picks are Tooltrace for photo-to-Gridfinity trays, Prusa EasyPrint for everyday mobile slicing, and 3D Filament Profiles for filament research and inventory; he is much more cautious about GridPilot, JusPrin, and preFlight.

What this video offers

Tooltrace moved to the top of the creator's Gridfinity recommendations after recent improvements to its AI tracing, automatic bin sizing, multi-tool workflow, and interface. He describes the Pro tier as about $8/month and says the free tier is useful.

GridPilot offers more granular controls but is still buggy and clunkier. The creator encourages feedback and says its creator has been responsive.

Tracefinity is a strong open-source, locally runnable alternative with more customization options.

BumpMesh can make models much more visually interesting, but detailed textures can multiply print time by two to three times or more.

Prusa EasyPrint covers roughly 80% of ordinary slicing needs and is the creator's default away from his office; he says it is best for supported printers and routine models rather than advanced tuning.

JusPrin's AI settings were mostly logical in testing but overdid infill, perimeters, and supports. The creator would currently recommend it mainly as a beginner learning tool.

FullSpectrum can use dithering to create many perceived colors, but the creator says a toolchanger makes the workflow much more practical.

preFlight advertises interesting advanced features, but the creator found profile migration and the interface disappointing and returned to OrcaSlicer.

3D Filament Profiles is his standout filament resource, with over 26,000 filaments, 48 material types, nearly 1,000 brands, settings, data sheets, color data, labels, and personal inventory tools.

FiLibrary is the creator's early-stage reference site for understanding filament types, strengths, weaknesses, and real-world examples.
